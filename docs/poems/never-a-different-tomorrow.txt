Among the austerities of our era
I missed the public provision of labyrinths.
The shopping centres tried. The car parks did

Get lost, they said. As though it were that easy.

Her apartment sounded promising

Evening unlettered the city. Upper windows

How much of what I knew was only lighting?
The red bricks went one-colour.

I hadn’t known how handy

A ship took the window’s light

Wanting something to last is not the same
